User Personas

Persona 1: Business Traveler (Rajesh, 35)

  • Travels Indore↔Bhopal weekly for work
  • Needs reliable, comfortable sedan
  • Books 1-2 days in advance
  • Pays via UPI, needs GST invoice
  • Values punctuality and AC

Persona 2: Family Pilgrim (Sunita, 50)

  • Group trips to Ujjain/Omkareshwar
  • Needs SUV for 5-6 people
  • Books for weekends/festivals
  • Wants multi-stop (temple circuit)
  • Husband handles UPI payment

Persona 3: Student/Young Professional (Amit, 24)

  • Weekend trips, airport rides
  • Price-sensitive, compares options
  • Books on phone, pays UPI
  • Wants live tracking shared with family
  • Rates and reviews every ride

Persona 4: Corporate Admin (Priya, 30)

  • Books cabs for company executives
  • Needs invoicing and receipt
  • Advance booking mandatory
  • Multiple bookings per week
  • Wants booking management dashboard

Market Size Estimation

TAM (Total Addressable Market)

  • India intercity cab market: ~$5-6B (subset of $24B taxi market)
  • MP state share: ~3-4% = $150-240M

SAM (Serviceable Addressable Market)

  • Indore intercity + intracity organized cab: ~₹200-500 Cr/year
  • Digital booking segment: 70% = ₹140-350 Cr/year

SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market)

  • Year 1 target: 1 car, ~10-15 rides/month
  • Revenue: ₹2,500 avg/ride × 12 rides × 12 months = ₹3.6 lakh/year
  • Year 2 (5 cars): ₹18-25 lakh/year
  • Year 3 (15 cars): ₹60-80 lakh/year

Target Demographics

  • Age: 25-55 years
  • Income: Middle class and above
  • Tech: Smartphone users with UPI
  • Location: Indore city and surrounding districts
  • Use case: Intercity travel (60%), airport transfers (25%), local (15%)
