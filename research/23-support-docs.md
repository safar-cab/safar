## Feedback Collection

### After Every Ride
- Star rating (1-5) — mandatory
- Written review — optional
- Quick tags: "On time", "Clean car", "Polite driver", "Safe driving"

### Monthly NPS Survey
- "How likely are you to recommend us?" (0-10)
- Sent via push notification to users with 3+ rides
- Track NPS trend monthly

### Bug Reporting
- In-app: "Report an issue" button
- Email: bugs@yourdomain.in
- Admin dashboard: Issue tracker
