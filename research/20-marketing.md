# Marketing Strategy

## Pre-Launch (Week 1-2 of Development)

### 1. Google Business Profile
- Create listing: "YourBrand - Cab Service in Indore"
- Add photos, services, contact number
- Free, shows up in Google Maps searches
- Cost: ₹0

### 2. JustDial Listing
- Free business listing
- "Cab service Indore", "Outstation cab Indore"
- Add phone number, website
- Cost: ₹0

### 3. WhatsApp Business
- Business profile with catalog
- Quick replies for common routes + pricing
- Status updates with offers
- Broadcast lists for repeat customers
- Cost: ₹0

### 4. Social Media Setup
- Instagram: @yourbrand (car photos, route highlights, customer reviews)
- Facebook Page: Same content + local community groups
- Post 3-5 times/week (routes, pricing, testimonials)
- Cost: ₹0

---

## Launch Strategy (Week of Launch)

### 1. Personal Network Launch
- Share with friends, family, colleagues
- Request first 5-10 bookings from known people
- Collect genuine 5-star reviews
- Word of mouth is strongest in Tier-2 cities

### 2. WhatsApp Campaigns
- Share launch message in local WhatsApp groups
- Indore city groups, travel groups, corporate groups
- Offer: "First ride 10% off" or "₹200 cashback on first ride"

### 3. Local Partnerships
- **Hotels/Hostels**: Leave cards/pamphlets, referral commission
- **Travel agents**: Outstation booking referrals
- **Corporate offices**: IT parks in Indore, monthly tie-ups
- **Temples**: Ujjain/Omkareshwar temple area partnerships
- **Airport**: Counter or pamphlets (if allowed)

## Referral Program

### User Referral
- User shares referral code
- New user gets ₹100 off first ride
- Referrer gets ₹100 off next ride
- Track via referral code in booking

### Driver Referral
- Driver refers another driver
- Both get ₹500 bonus after 10 completed rides

## Seasonal Opportunities

| Season | Opportunity |
|--------|------------|
| Navratri (Oct) | Ujjain temple trips surge |
| Diwali (Nov) | Family trips, hometown travel |
| Wedding Season (Nov-Feb) | Group bookings, SUV demand |
| Summer (Apr-Jun) | Hill station trips from Indore |
| Monsoon (Jul-Sep) | Less demand, offer discounts |
| Simhastha Kumbh (every 12 years) | Massive Ujjain traffic |
