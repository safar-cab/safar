## Domain Strategy
- Register `.in` for India market (₹99-199/year at BigRock)
- `.co.in` even cheaper (~₹49/year) but looks less professional
- Skip `.com` initially — expensive and likely taken
- BigRock offers `.in` for ₹9/year on 3-year plan

## Brand Vibe
- **Trustworthy** — Document-verified drivers and cars
- **Simple** — Book in 3 taps
- **Transparent** — Fixed pricing, no surge, no cash
- **Safe** — Real-time GPS tracking, emergency contacts

## Logo Direction
- Clean, minimal icon (car silhouette + route line)
- Color palette: Blue (trust) + Orange (energy) or Green (go/safety)
- Admin can change logo and company name from settings

## Tagline Ideas
- "Your ride, your route"
- "Travel smarter, pay digital"
- "Book. Ride. Track."
- "Safe rides, transparent fares"
