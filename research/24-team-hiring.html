Driver Recruitment

Requirements

  • Valid commercial driving license
  • Clean driving record
  • Smartphone with data plan
  • Local references
  • Know local routes (Indore + MP highways)

Where to Find

  • Existing taxi driver networks
  • Auto driver associations
  • Employment offices (Rozgar offices)
  • Word of mouth
  • Local newspaper classifieds

Onboarding

  1. Document verification (license, Aadhaar)
  2. Background check (police verification)
  3. Driving test (1-hour evaluation)
  4. App training (2 hours)
  5. Customer service training (1 hour)
  6. Trial rides (3 supervised rides)
